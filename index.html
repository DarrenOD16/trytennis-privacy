Privacy notice: how we keep families' details

Last updated 27 November 2026

Who we are

TRY TENNIS is run by Wackit.Net Limited (company number 07340265, registered office 5a Ack Lane East, Bramhall, Stockport, Cheshire SK7 2BE). We provide tennis coaching, courses and camps, mainly at Bramhall Queensgate Tennis Club (BQTC).

Records, our shared system

We keep the details of the families we coach, and of the people on our email list, in Records, a secure system we share with BQTC. Many of our families are also club members, so one accurate record per person is safer than two lists. TRY TENNIS and BQTC are jointly responsible for these shared records under a written agreement:

  • each of us uses them only for our own purposes, set out below;
  • either of us can help you with your rights;
  • we tell each other promptly if anything goes wrong.

What we keep

  • names;
  • email and mobile;
  • children's dates of birth or school year;
  • who is in your family and who may act for a child;
  • whether a child has a medical condition (yes or no);
  • your email preferences;
  • the coaching groups a child attends;
  • if you enquire: what you asked about, where your enquiry is up to, follow-up dates and our notes;
  • if you're on our email list: your name, email address, when you joined, your email preferences and the topics (tags) we use to send you relevant news;
  • for club members, your membership details;
  • if you message us on WhatsApp: your messages, your WhatsApp number and name, and when each message was delivered and read (and, once we start keeping them, any photos or voice notes you send us).

We don't keep medical details unless you choose to give them in our app, and only a few staff can see them.

Who sees it

  • Only TRY TENNIS's and BQTC's owners and administrators, signing in with a password and a two-step code.
  • Coaches don't see it.
  • In our app you see only your own family.
  • Each organisation looks at the other's people only when it needs to.
  • Enquiries are each organisation's own: only its own owners and administrators see them.
  • WhatsApp messages are TRY TENNIS's alone: only TRY TENNIS staff see them. We talk to parents, and don't knowingly message children directly.

Who helps us

  • Supabase stores Records securely in London, UK.
  • Mailchimp (USA) sends our emails, under the UK transfer safeguards in its data processing terms.
  • Stripe handles card payments; we never see or store card details.
  • WhatsApp (run by Meta, in Ireland and the USA) carries the messages you send us on WhatsApp, under its WhatsApp Business terms and their UK transfer safeguards. Until we finish moving off it, Make (an automation service, in Europe) also passes some messages on to us.
  • We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • coaching families: 2 years after your last session or booking;
  • enquiries that don't lead to a booking: 2 years after our last contact;
  • club members (the club's records): while a member, plus 2 years;
  • a record of your email permission: while you're on our list, plus 2 years;
  • a history of changes (with no free text): 6 years;
  • WhatsApp messages: as long as the rest of your record, and removed with it; messages from a number we can't match to anyone: 2 years after the last message.

After that, the record is anonymised: names and contact details are removed.

Your rights

You can ask to see the details we hold, have them corrected, have them deleted (we'll anonymise them), or stop our emails. Ask either organisation:

TRY TENNIS: Darren, darren@trytennis.com

BQTC: Darren, the club's privacy contact, darren@trytennis.com

We reply within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
